How it works
• Part 1: Mass of one atom and of a diatomic molecule (10 pts)
• Part 2: Molecular mass of ionic compounds, organic compounds and compounds with many atoms (10 pts)
• Part 3: Percent composition of an element in a compound (10 pts)
• Each part starts with a worked example and the formula to use. The example stays available while you answer.
• Each question is worth 1 point and has one attempt. Use the atomic masses table (🧪 button, top right).
• Answers may be rounded to 2 decimal places. Masses are in amu.
• Use the keyboard, mouse, pen, or touch keypad. Press Enter to check.
